# Generator review, 2026-09-11 (special edition)

Part 2 of the special edition comparing the cheap and free arms against
four frontier models run as primaries. Written by hand from the record
(generator 2.14.0 was not run: the edition draws on no new catalog capture,
so the content pass had nothing to select from that issue 2 had not).
Proposed edits are proposals; nothing here has been applied.

## Triggers fired

None. No capture was made, so T1, T4, T7 and T9 were not evaluated
mechanically; nothing observed while writing suggests any of them moved
since 2026-09-10.

## Triggers checked and not fired

- **T5** (observed). Still no public benchmark that measures review quality.
  The four frontier runs on the zero-defect control are this survey's own
  measurement, n=1 each.
- **T6** (observed). No card contradiction. Two facts that are not
  contradictions but that a card does not carry: `openai/gpt-5.6-terra-pro`
  counts prompt tokens at about three times the payload on every run, and
  `google/gemini-3.8-flash` reasons long enough to bill at Pro prices on a
  Flash listing. Both are in the edition as items, not as caveats.
- **T8** (observed). oxbox 1.3.0 ran every request; nothing in the
  workflow changed.
- **T10** (observed). Twelve primary runs, one resend, eight metered checks.

## Shape questions the special edition raises

The edition keeps the eight section headings of `docs/issue-shape.md`
verbatim and drops the catalog, stealth and churn parts of the record,
because nothing was captured. Three departures for the editor to rule on,
each carried into the review Doc's ask list:

1. **The line under the title** reads `Special edition, 2026-09-11.` rather
   than `Issue N, YYYY-MM-DD.` The site takes that line as the archive
   card's summary, so the wording is what a reader sees in the archive.
2. **A third paragraph after the fixed two-sentence block** says what the
   edition asks. The shape says the block is fixed words; a reader arriving
   at a special edition needs the one sentence that says why it is not a
   weekly.
3. **The record opens with "The arms"**, a part the shape does not name: one
   four-column table of every model the comparison draws on, with price,
   control count and run-plus-check total. If the editor wants special
   editions to have a shape of their own, this is the candidate for it.

## A maintainer's note, not for the issue

`corpora/scorers/ask_grounding.py` returned FAIL on three of the four
frontier grounding runs and every one was 10 of 10 by hand: Opus 5 wrote its
answers under `## N.` headings the splitter does not recognize, and "429s"
and "900 seconds" in two correct answers matched the number patterns. The
scorer's verdict is recorded beside the reader's in each observation. A
splitter that accepts a heading form, and a bad-pattern that ignores a
number the answer itself calls the wrong one, would have made all four
mechanical. Left as is: the scorer is the survey's tooling, and the rule is
that tooling notes stay out of the issue.

## Proposed edits

None to `SKILL.md`. The three shape questions above are for
`docs/issue-shape.md`, and only if the editor wants special editions to
