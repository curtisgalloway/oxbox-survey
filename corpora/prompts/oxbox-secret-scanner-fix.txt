The scan in `ox` decides whether a local file is published to a third-party model
endpoint, so a pattern that fails to match is a leaked credential, not a cosmetic miss.

Measured against SECRET_PATTERNS as it stands, one assignment form per line. The values
are written as <n chars> rather than spelled out, so that this task text does not itself
trip the scanner:

    caught   api_key = "<16 chars, double-quoted>"
    missed   client_secret=<20 chars, unquoted>
    missed   my_api_key = "<20 chars, double-quoted>"
    missed   DB_PASSWORD=<21 chars, unquoted>
    missed   token: <36 chars, unquoted, a JWT>
    missed   aws_secret_access_key=<40 chars of A-Za-z0-9/+=, unquoted>

Make every missed form match. These two must keep matching nothing, because ox is
routinely asked to read its own source and a scanner that fires on them refuses every
run:

    clean    "max_tokens": 100000
    clean    completion_tokens = 512

Change the pattern list only. Do not change how the scan is called, what it reports, or
what the caller does with a hit.
