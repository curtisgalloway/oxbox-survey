# Gemini 3.7 Flash baseline on the scanner fix: applies clean, 8 of 8 verdicts, zero self-hits, after 38,000 tokens of reasoning

**What happened** — `oxbox-secret-scanner-fix` (39,467 B, `ox` at `6072d56`, `--mode
diff`, `--max-tokens 65536` because that is this model's cap) put to
`google/gemini-3.7-flash`. The patch passes all three mechanical gates, the first
run against this fixture to do so. It spent 38,213 of its 38,609 completion tokens
reasoning — 336,470 characters — to produce a three-bullet explanation and a
one-line pattern change.

## Evidence

Run `logs/2026-09-03T02-29-45Z`, `context_bytes` 39467, `finish_reason` stop,
`truncated` false, `ox_version` 0.5.0. Scored with
`corpora/scorers/secret_scanner_fix.py`:

```
gate 1  APPLY   PASS  git apply --check clean
gate 2  SCAN    8 of 8 verdicts hold
gate 3  SELF    PASS  zero hits over ox at the pin and as patched
scope   in contract: nothing outside SECRET_PATTERNS changed
patterns: 8 before, 8 after
RESULT  PASS
```

The replacement pattern:

```
(?i)["']?\b[a-z0-9_.\-]*(?:key|secret|password|token)\b["']?\s*[:=]\s*
(?:["'][^"'\s]{16,}["']|[A-Za-z0-9_.\-/+=]{16,})
```

Its hunk header is `@@ -77,8 +77,8 @@` against a list that starts at line 69; the
offset is wrong and the counts are right, which is the combination `git apply`
tolerates. The identifier half now matches anything *ending* in `key`, `secret`,
`password` or `token`, wider than the task asked for, and the `\b` after the
keyword is what keeps `max_tokens` and `completion_tokens` out. Gate 3 confirms
that on the whole of `ox`; it does not test any other file, and a broader net
than the reference fix's is a real trade the mechanical gates cannot see.

## So what

Same fixture, same bytes, two frontier models, two different failure profiles:
Sonnet 5 wrote a tighter pattern with a wrong hunk header and one self-hit;
Gemini Flash wrote a looser pattern that clears every gate. The mechanical
fixture ranks them the other way from what a reader would guess from the price
tags, and that is the point of having one. It also says what a free model has to
beat here: a clean apply and 8 of 8 is now the measured bar, not an aspiration.

Reasoning volume is the cost story. At $3.75 per million output tokens this run
computed to $0.15, all of it thinking; the visible answer is under 700 characters.

No marker: this is a baseline, not a candidate.
