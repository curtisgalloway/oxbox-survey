# Gemini 3.8 Flash baseline on the zero-defect control: two findings, one real, one invented, after 40,231 reasoning tokens and 237 seconds

**What happened** — `oxbox-clean-control` (`jailtest.py` at `6302b12`,
6,096 B, `--mode review`, effort high, 100,000 tokens, temperature 0.2) put
to `google/gemini-3.8-flash`. Two findings. The first is the stat oracle on
`/etc/shadow`, the record's G1, with the correct cause (`stat()` needs only
search permission on `/etc`) and one wrong clause ("sorted first") beside a
right one ("the only path present in a minimal CI/container environment").

numbers: **emitted 2, genuinely new and real 1, inventions 1.** It found
