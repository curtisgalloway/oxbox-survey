# GPT-5.6 sol baseline on the scanner fix: passes all three gates, 2,001 completion tokens, 35 seconds

**What happened** — `oxbox-secret-scanner-fix` (39,467 B, `ox` at `6072d56`, `--mode
diff`) put to `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. Clean apply, 8 of 8 verdicts, zero self-hits,
nothing changed outside the pattern list. It did it in 2,001 completion tokens
against Gemini Flash's 38,609 and Sonnet's 15,479 for the same payload.

## Evidence

Run `logs/2026-09-03T02-41-05Z`, `context_bytes` 39467, `finish_reason` stop.
`corpora/scorers/secret_scanner_fix.py`:


The pattern:

```
(?i)\b(?:[a-z0-9]+[_\-])*(?:api[_\-]?key|secret(?:[_\-]access[_\-]key)?|password|token)\b
\s*[:=]\s*(?:["'][^"'\s]{16,}["']|[^\s"']{16,})
```

The hunk header `@@ -72,8 +72,8 @@ SECRET_PATTERNS = [` has the wrong start line
and the right counts, like Gemini's. Of the three, this is the pattern closest in
shape to the reference fix `6ba47d8`: it keeps the keyword list explicit and
extends it with `secret_access_key` rather than matching any identifier that ends
in a keyword. Its unquoted-value class `[^\s"']{16,}` is the widest of the three,
which the gates cannot penalise; nothing in `ox` at the pin trips it.

The explanation's third line — "the token-count settings remain clean because
their numeric values are shorter than the credential threshold" — is the correct
account of why `max_tokens = DEFAULT_MAX_TOKENS` is *not* covered by that
reasoning, and yet the pattern survives it because `max_tokens` does not match
`\b(?:[a-z0-9]+[_\-])*token\b`: the `\b` after `token` fails against `s`. The
model's stated reason is incomplete; its pattern is right anyway.

## So what

Three frontier baselines, three profiles on one fixture: Sonnet 5 fails on a hunk
header and a self-hit, Gemini Flash passes at 38k tokens, GPT-5.6 sol passes at
2k. Cost on this fixture ran Sonnet $0.18, Gemini $0.15, GPT $0.04, computed.
The measured bar for a free model here is a clean apply and 8 of 8; the measured
efficiency bar is two thousand tokens.

No marker: this is a baseline, not a candidate.
