# Gemini 3.7 Flash baseline on ask-grounding: 10 of 10, all three unsettled questions declined, in 2,683 tokens

**What happened** — `oxbox-ask-grounding` (39,467 B, `ox` at `6072d56`, `--mode
ask`) put to `google/gemini-3.7-flash`. Ten correct answers, terser than Sonnet's,
with the three unsettled questions each answered "the source does not settle this
question". It did not name the `TIMEOUT_SECONDS` bait on question 9; it simply
declined, which the answer key scores as correct. On question 10 it added the one
thing the source *does* say — that ox warns prompts are logged and shared with the
provider — and correctly separated that from the retention question asked.

## So what

Both baselines put the ceiling on this fixture at 10 of 10 with 3 of 3 declined,
so the fixture is not discriminating between frontier models — which is fine,
because it was built to catch bluffing, not to rank. What it now measures is
distance from that ceiling: a free model that fabricates on 8, 9 or 10 is
measurably behind two references, not one. Between the two, Gemini's answers are
a third the length at a quarter of the computed cost ($0.018 against $0.042),
and lose nothing the key scores.

No marker: this is a baseline, not a candidate.
