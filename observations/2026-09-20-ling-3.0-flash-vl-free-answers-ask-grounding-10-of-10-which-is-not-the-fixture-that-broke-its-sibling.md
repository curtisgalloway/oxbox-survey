# ling-3.0-flash-vl:free answers ask-grounding 10 of 10 — which is not the fixture that broke its sibling

**What happened** — the second of the four free rows OpenRouter added between
the 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-20 catalogs, and the vision variant of a model
already in the record. It answered the fixture 10 of 10 for nothing, through
Novita, in 23 seconds.

The reason this row was worth running is weaker than it looked. The sibling
`inclusionai/ling-3.0-flash-fin:free` is the model `answered: false` was added
for — but it returned nothing on the **zero-defect control**, not on
ask-grounding, where it scored 10 of 10 in 8 seconds on 2026-09-08. So this run
does not test the thing that broke the sibling. It establishes the row and
nothing more.

- **q10** — "The source does not directly address whether the provider uses

That is 10 of 10.

## So what

A free row that answers, reads a 39 KB file accurately, and cites a line
number correctly enough to be checked. It costs nothing and took 23 seconds.

It is also the fifth model in two days to score 10 of 10 on this fixture.
ask-grounding-v2 is saturated: it was demoted to a smoke test on 2026-09-08 and
every model run since — glm-5.3-flash twice, deepseek-v4-flash paid and free,
and now this — has scored full marks. It certifies that a model reads a file
and answers questions about it. It no longer discriminates, and a rating cannot
be built on it.

**The experiment worth running next** is the one this row does not do: the
zero-defect control, where the sibling spent its entire 32,000-token cap
reasoning and returned nothing. The v2 fixture exists precisely because of that
failure — an 8,000-token cap at the vendors' sampling — so whether this variant
exhausts it the same way is a real question with a recorded prior, and it is a
harder fixture than the one run here.

2,719 completion tokens, no cache hit. The checking half is this agent's
session and carries no costcheck window: the scorer is mechanical and the three
reader questions were settled by reading three line ranges of the pinned file.
