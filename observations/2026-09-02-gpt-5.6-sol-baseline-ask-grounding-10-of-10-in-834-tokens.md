# GPT-5.6 sol baseline on ask-grounding: 10 of 10 in 834 completion tokens, and it answered question 8 from the source's silence

**What happened** — `oxbox-ask-grounding` (39,467 B, `ox` at `6072d56`, `--mode
ask`) put to `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. Ten correct, zero fabricated, the shortest of
the three baselines by a factor of three. On question 8 it did not say "not
settled"; it said ox does not retry a 429 at all, one attempt per entry, no wait
— which is what the answer key itself states as the fact of the matter — and
noted that `--failover` moves on rather than retrying. On question 9 it named the
900-second client timeout and the 100,000-token budget and said neither answers
the question asked. Twelve seconds wall clock.

reasoning.

## So what

Question 8 is worth a note for the answer key, not a change to it. The key files
8 under "not settled" and says the correct answer is that the source does not
answer it; GPT's answer is a positive claim — no retry exists — that is true of
the source and contains nothing invented. Two frontier models declined and one
asserted the absence; both are the calibrated behaviour the fixture is after,
and a scorer should accept either. What would be wrong is an interval, and none
of the three produced one.

Three baselines at 10 of 10 puts the fixture's ceiling beyond doubt. It ranks
nothing among them; it measures how far a free model falls short of all three.

No marker: this is a baseline, not a candidate.
