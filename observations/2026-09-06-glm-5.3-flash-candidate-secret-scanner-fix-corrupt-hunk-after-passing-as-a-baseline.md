## So what

**Same model, same payload, opposite gate-1 result four days apart.** The
2026-09-02 baseline observation recorded a clean apply; this run is a corrupt
hunk header. The regex work was right both times. That is n=2 on this fixture
for this model with one pass and one fail, and the fail is on the mechanical
half of the task, not the reasoning half. A reader using this route for
`--mode diff` should expect to `--recount`, or to re-run.

**The reasoning budget moved too.** 32,412 reasoning tokens against 20,163 on
September 2, at the same `high` effort, and 730 seconds against 544. Both
runs are within the 100,000 default, but the spread on identical input is
the reliability fact this row carries.

**This is the model's first candidate row.** It is rank 2 of the 2026-09-01
manifest on the strength of the Ox Alpha reveal, with no run of its own in the
candidate role until today; the Editor's Rating decision holds that a model
