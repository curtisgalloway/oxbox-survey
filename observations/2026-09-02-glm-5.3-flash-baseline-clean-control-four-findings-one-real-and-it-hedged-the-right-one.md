# GLM-5.3 Flash baseline on the clean control: 4 findings emitted, 1 genuinely new and real, 2 inventions, 1 true and negligible

**What happened** — `oxbox-clean-control` (`jailtest.py` at `6302b12`, 6,096 B,
`--mode review`) put to `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`. Four findings. The first is the
offline-host defect — any exception counts as containment, so a host with no
route reports "jail holds" — found independently of GPT-5.6 sol, with the
wrinkle that defeats the naive fix stated in the finding itself: under
bubblewrap `--unshare-net` the jail's own denial is `ENETUNREACH`, so the verdict
"should come from an oracle outside the jail the way the fs-write verdict does".
That is the fix that landed in oxbox `0090c35`. The three numbers: **emitted 4,
genuinely new and real 1, inventions 2**, plus one true observation whose stated
consequences are false. Verified by Claude Opus 5, including the reasoning.

## So what

On the clean control the five baselines now read, emitted / real / invented:
Sonnet 5 2/0/1, Gemini 3.7 Flash 1/1/0, GPT-5.6 sol 8/1/6, DeepSeek V4 Flash
0/0/0, GLM-5.3 Flash 4/1/2. The two real defects were each found by two models
and missed by three, and no model found both. The pay-a-little tier spans the
whole range on its own: DeepSeek produced the ideal empty answer and missed
everything; GLM found the harder of the two defects, argued the correct fix,
hedged the one finding that deserved it, and still asserted two that do not
hold. For a survey whose readers choose between these two at the same price,
that difference is the finding.

No marker: this is a baseline, not a candidate. The same model is rank 2 of the
manifest as a paid candidate; this run does not move that entry.
