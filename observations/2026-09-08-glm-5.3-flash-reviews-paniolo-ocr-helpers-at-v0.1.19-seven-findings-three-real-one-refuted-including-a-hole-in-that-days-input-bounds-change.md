evening (`2026-09-07T23-50-02Z`), now read from the checkout at tag
v0.1.19 (`10da130`) after the bounds and clipping fixes (paniolo PRs #163
and #164) had landed: `ocr/linuxocr`, `ocr/rapidocr`, `ocr/visionocr.swift`,

ran 383 s. A Sonnet 5 subagent verified every finding against the pin.

## So what

pre-fix run re-derived from the issue tracker are gone, the two it added
remain, and it found one new real defect in the fix itself plus one real
error-swallowing bug in the Swift helper. The refuted claim was a
misreading of a comment, settled by arithmetic. At 52 KB the batch sat
above the skill's 40 KB guideline and still came back complete; reasoning
tokens (17,922) dominated the completion.
