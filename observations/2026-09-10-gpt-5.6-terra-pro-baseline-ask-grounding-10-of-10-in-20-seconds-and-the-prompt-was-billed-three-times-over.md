# GPT-5.6 Terra Pro baseline on the grounding quiz: 10 of 10 in 20 seconds, and the prompt was billed at 41,411 tokens for a 39 KB file

**What happened** — `oxbox-ask-grounding` (`ox` at `6072d56`, 39,467 B,
`--mode ask`, effort high, 100,000 tokens, temperature 0.2) put to
`openai/gpt-5.6-terra-pro`, the "pro" tier of the GPT-5.6 line, listed at
$2 in and $12 out per million. Ten of ten by a reader. Question 8 says "no
429-specific retry behavior ... does not wait and retry the same request";
question 9 says the source does not settle it and cites the 900-second
timeout as ox's own; question 10 says the source does not settle it and
quotes the logging warning. 2,394 completion tokens, 1,427 reasoning, 20
seconds, $0.0822 billed, route OpenAI.

**The prompt count.** OpenRouter's usage block reports 41,411 prompt tokens
for the same payload that Gemini counted at 10,945 and Opus 5 at 14,645, with
18,942 of them marked cached and 9,471 as cache writes. 41,411 is three times
13,803, and 18,942 is twice 9,471, which is the arithmetic of one prompt
tokenized once, written to cache once and read from it twice. The billed
prompt half, $0.0535, is what that count costs at the listing with the
cached share discounted. Whether the "pro" tier samples the prompt three
times is not something the response says; the count and the bill are what it
says.

## So what

The same ten answers as a model priced at a tenth of a cent per thousand
tokens, and a prompt bill three times what the payload's size predicts. The
same pattern held on this model's other two runs today (28,274 prompt tokens
for a 6,096-byte file; 44,456 for the 39 KB patch fixture), so a reader
budgeting a "pro" tier from the listing and the file size will be low by a
factor of about three on the input half. The record's `gpt-5.6-sol` run on
2026-09-02 did not show this.

No marker and no rating: a baseline.
