# GPT-5.6 Terra Pro baseline on the zero-defect control: five findings, one real, four invented, the most expensive run on this file at 31 cents

**What happened** — `oxbox-clean-control` (`jailtest.py` at `6302b12`,
6,096 B, `--mode review`, effort high, 100,000 tokens, temperature 0.2) put
to `openai/gpt-5.6-terra-pro`. Five findings, each a file-and-lines bullet
with a failure scenario. The first is the probe that scores every
exception as containment, the record's L1, with a host firewall and a
dead resolver as the triggers. The other four need a launcher, a policy or
a work directory this tree does not have. It did not find G1. The three
numbers: **emitted 5, genuinely new and real 1, inventions 4.** 21,310
completion tokens, 19,440 reasoning, 147 seconds, $0.3061 billed, route
OpenAI; prompt tokens 28,274 for a 6,096-byte file, 3,454 of them cached,
the threefold count this model showed on all three runs today.

The two metered checkers agree on three of five: TP1 confirmed, TP3 and
TP4 refuted. Fable 5.1 confirmed TP2 and TP5; Opus 5 refuted both. The
record goes with Opus 5 on both and says why.
