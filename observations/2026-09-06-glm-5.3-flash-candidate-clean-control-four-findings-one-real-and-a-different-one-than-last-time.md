# GLM-5.3 Flash as a candidate on the clean control: 4 findings, 1 real, 3 inventions, and the real one is the other known defect this time, billed at double the catalog price by a different provider

**What happened** — `oxbox-clean-control` (`jailtest.py` at `6302b12`, 6,096 B,
`--mode review`) put to `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` as a candidate. Four findings.
The one that holds is the stat oracle firing on `/etc/shadow`, Gemini's G1,
hedged UNCERTAIN and reached by a partial mechanism: the model blames uid 0,
where the actual cause is bubblewrap binding `/etc` so the stat succeeds at
any uid, but the scenario as written is reachable and the consequence, a
false FAIL, is right. As a baseline four days earlier the same model found
the other known defect, the offline host, and missed this one. The three
numbers: **emitted 4, genuinely new and real 1, inventions 3**, two of the
three hedged. It declined both pre-registered false positives.

OpenRouter routed this run to SiliconFlow rather than Z.AI, which served the
other two runs the same afternoon, and billed $0.0034 against a catalog
computation of $0.0017. The billed figure is recorded; see the cost section.

Verified by Fable 5.1 against the pin and the verdict key. Every verdict
cites what settles it.

## So what

**Same model, same payload, a different real defect each time.** As a
baseline it found the offline-host defect and missed the stat oracle; as a
candidate it found the stat oracle and missed the offline host. One real of
four both times, and two of the three inventions today are the same two it
made on September 2, one of them promoted from hedged to asserted. n=2 says
this model reaches one of the two known defects per run and repeats its
inventions.

**The same model id was billed at two prices on one afternoon.** The ask and
diff runs went to Z.AI at the catalog's $0.075 in and $0.25 out; this one went
to SiliconFlow and the upstream cost line reads $0.000258 for 1,720 prompt
tokens and $0.00314 for 6,280 completion, which is $0.15 and $0.50 per
million, double the card. A catalog price is the price of one route, and
OpenRouter chooses the route. `usd_model` records what was billed.

**This completes the model's three candidate rows.** With the scanner fix
(quality 0, corrupt hunk) and ask-grounding (5, 5, 5) it is now ratable under
the Editor's Rating decision. The verification half here is a shared session
window, so no `usd_total` and no cost digit on this fixture.
